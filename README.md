# Dukes of Beauty — Noir & Champagne Editorial Website

A new salon website design inspired by the first Dukes of Beauty website's **luxury black, champagne-gold, ivory and photographic style**. This version is a new composition—not a copy of the original.

## What is different
- Cinematic full-width real salon imagery with editorial gold typography.
- New layered salon photo frame and restrained pointer-responsive 3D depth.
- Scrolling treatment ribbon, two-photo atelier story, premium treatment collection and signature typography section.
- Warm ivory complete treatment menu and photo gallery for visual contrast.
- Refined WhatsApp booking form, authentic Google reviews link, and contact section.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences; responsive desktop and mobile layout.

## Pricing & booking safeguards
- The existing **111 treatments in seven categories** are sourced from the earlier Dukes catalog and preserved with previously reconciled entries.
- Services may be selected from `src/App.tsx` and pass through the unchanged, working `src/Booking.tsx` WhatsApp request experience.
- Final appointments are **not** confirmed by the website and real-time availability is **not** represented as verified.
- Estimates never invent an upper limit for prices labelled “from”. The salon confirms any final price.
- No invented Google review quotes, ratings, stock salon pictures presented as client work, or artificial service results.
